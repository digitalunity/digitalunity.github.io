Digital Unity technology · Hexjack Lens

See the bigger picture. Get to the right answer.

Hexjack Lens is a Secure-by-Design, read-oriented MCP server that gives AI systems controlled, evidence-backed access to an organisation's information estate.

Current engineering stage · September 2026

The information and investigation foundations are implemented.

The Go/MCP foundation, research-context registry, runtime credential boundary, destination-bound source contract, Jira/Confluence and Bitbucket adapters, local Git-mirror repository retrieval, generic file/document sources, common evidence model, provider-neutral investigation contract, authenticated investigation transport, maintained threat model, controlled software-supply-chain pipeline and supported OCI/native deployment baseline are implemented and verified. Remaining V1 work completes the final public MCP surface, Hexjack operator lifecycle, deployment-level network/egress controls, operational-security controls and customer verification.

The information estate

Your organisation already knows more than any single AI can see.

Useful knowledge is distributed across documentation, issue trackers, source repositories, operational systems and other approved sources. Lens provides a controlled research layer between AI and that information.

Instead of teaching every AI client how every underlying system works, organisations configure their information topology once behind a bounded interface.

One Lens, bounded capabilities

Search

Implemented source adapters can search configured organisational systems and repository mirrors without exposing arbitrary destinations.

Retrieve

Implemented retrieval reads bounded source evidence while retaining source identity, context and provenance.

Investigate

The provider-neutral investigation contract and its authenticated, destination-bound HTTPS/Unix transport are implemented beneath the public surface. The final MCP investigation operation remains a separate V1 delivery step.

Evidence first

Know what was retrieved, what was observed and what was inferred.

Lens is designed to preserve the distinction between recorded organisational evidence, observations established during authorised investigation and conclusions inferred from that evidence. Provenance tells an AI where information came from without turning every retrieved document into a trusted instruction.
