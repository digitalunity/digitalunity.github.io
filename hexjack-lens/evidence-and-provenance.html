Hexjack Lens · Evidence & provenance

Evidence first. Conclusions in context.

Lens is designed so an AI can distinguish information retrieved from an organisational source from something observed during investigation or inferred from the available evidence.

Recorded / retrieved

Information read directly from a configured source such as documentation, a ticket or repository file.

Observed

A finding directly established by an authorised investigation environment from an artefact or environment.

Inference

A reasoned conclusion derived from evidence, kept distinguishable from the evidence that supports it.

Provenance

Where did this answer come from?

Useful AI answers should remain connected to their evidence. Lens carries source and context identity and, where applicable, document, path, reference, commit, environment and freshness information.

That makes it possible for a consumer to understand why a conclusion was reached and return to the underlying evidence.

A security property too

Information is evidence — not authority.

A document, ticket or source file may contain incorrect, malicious or misleading content. Provenance says where material came from; it does not grant that material permission to change Lens configuration, credentials, destinations or security boundaries.

Controlled research starts with controlled trust.
