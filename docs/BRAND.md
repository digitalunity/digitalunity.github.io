# Digital Unity Brand Usage

## Brand idea

Digital Unity's adopted primary mark is the geometric identity created by **Combine Studio in 2020**. The original Combine Studio work described the identity as a professional, modern digital mark built around the idea of **unity, automation and digital processes**.

In 2026 the mark has been retained but placed inside a more restrained visual system: clean typography, generous whitespace, charcoal/white foundations and a controlled orange accent.

The historic elephant / punctuation mark remains part of Digital Unity's brand history. It is no longer the primary identity because its silhouette can be read as too similar to Evernote's elephant mark.

## Primary identity

- Primary mark: Combine Studio geometric unity/digital device.
- Primary horizontal lock-up: orange Combine mark + orange `Digital` + charcoal `Unity` on light backgrounds.
- Dark-background lock-up: orange Combine mark + white `Digital Unity`.
- Mark-only usage: favicon, social/company avatar and small document device.
- Historic elephant mark: archive/history only unless a future explicit decision reinstates it.

The Combine mark geometry should be taken from the original 2020 vector artwork, not redrawn by eye.

## Colour

- Charcoal: `#141A21`
- Orange: `#FF5A00`
- Light grey: `#F2F3F4`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Orange is an accent, not a background system. The visual identity should remain predominantly neutral and restrained.

## Typography

Use **Inter** for the website and digital collateral where available. Use a clean system sans-serif fallback when Inter is unavailable.

- Headlines: Inter SemiBold / 600
- Subheads: Inter Medium or SemiBold
- Body: Inter Regular / 400

The current website's restraint is part of the brand. Do not change typography merely to make it feel more designed.

## Enduring line

**Easier to understand. Easier to run. Easier to improve.**

## Core principle

**Technology follows the problem.**

This is a proposition principle, not a decorative slogan to repeat everywhere.

## Documents

Client-facing documents should use the same approved primitives as the website: Combine Studio mark, Inter typography, charcoal/white foundations, selective orange accents and approved decorative graphics. Keep document content editable; do not flatten an entire page into one image and call it a template.

Substantial client deliverables can use dark charcoal covers, white internal pages and selective orange section numbers/rules/callouts.

## Website

Preserve the clean information architecture and layout. Brand changes should add recognition rather than trigger a design-system rewrite.

Allowed default treatments include the horizontal logo in the header, Combine mark favicon, selective orange hover/accent states and orange section numbering/rules.

Avoid gradients, stock-heavy agency imagery, oversized technology-logo walls and ornamental effects that compete with the proposition.

## Historical identity

### Historic elephant mark

The elephant/punctuation device was a long-running Digital Unity identity based on the idea that the systems recorded important information and "an elephant never forgets". It remains legitimate company history, but is retired from primary use because of its visual similarity to the Evernote elephant.

### 2020 Combine Studio identity

The Combine Studio geometric identity was commissioned in January 2020 and is now the adopted primary mark. The 2026 treatment keeps the mark while modernising the surrounding system rather than reinstating the whole 2020 visual style.

The adopted direction is therefore **continuity with refinement**: original Combine Studio geometry, current Digital Unity colour/typography, and the mature 2026 website as the surrounding visual language.
