# Digital Unity Brand Usage

## Brand idea

Digital Unity retains the elephant-derived punctuation mark from its historic identity. The original idea was simple: Digital Unity builds systems that record and preserve important business information, and **an elephant never forgets**.

The modern identity extends that idea without over-explaining it: remember what matters, make hidden operational knowledge explicit, and help organisations move forward with confidence.

The elephant is a brand mark, not a mascot. Do not add character names, cartoon treatments or forced elephant metaphors to public copy.

## Primary identity

- Primary mark: simplified elephant / punctuation device.
- Primary horizontal lock-up: orange elephant + orange `Digital` + charcoal `Unity` on light backgrounds.
- Dark-background lock-up: orange elephant + white `Digital Unity`.
- Mark-only usage: favicon, social/company avatar and small document device.

## Colour

- Charcoal: `#141A21`
- Orange: `#FF5A00`
- Light grey: `#F2F3F4`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Orange is an accent, not a background system. The visual identity should remain predominantly neutral and restrained.

## Typography

Use **Inter** for the website and digital collateral where available. Use a clean system sans-serif fallback when Inter is unavailable.

- Headlines: Inter SemiBold / 600
- Subheads: Inter Medium or SemiBold
- Body: Inter Regular / 400

The current website's restraint is part of the brand. Do not change typography merely to make it feel more designed.

## Enduring line

**Easier to understand. Easier to run. Easier to improve.**

## Core principle

**Technology follows the problem.**

This is a proposition principle, not a decorative slogan to repeat everywhere.

## Documents

Substantial client deliverables should normally use dark charcoal covers, white internal pages and selective orange section numbers/rules/callouts. The Operational Diagnosis report treatment is the reference direction.

## Website

Preserve the clean information architecture and layout. Brand changes should add recognition rather than trigger a design-system rewrite.

Allowed default treatments include the horizontal logo in the header, elephant favicon, selective orange hover/accent states and orange section numbering/rules.

Avoid gradients, stock-heavy agency imagery, oversized technology-logo walls and ornamental effects that compete with the proposition.

## Historical identity

The 2020 Combine Studio geometric identity is legitimate Digital Unity design history but is not the chosen current direction.

The adopted direction is **evolution, not replacement**: retain and simplify the historic elephant mark while using the mature 2026 website as the surrounding visual language.
